Who We Are

Training that builds a stronger team.

ETS is focused on helping members learn their roles, understand community standards, and grow through fair and consistent instruction.

01

Structured Learning

Organised sessions help personnel understand procedures, expectations, and the responsibilities of their roles.

02

Consistent Standards

Clear guidance helps create a shared standard of professionalism, discipline, and respectful conduct.

03

Continued Development

Feedback and practice give members opportunities to improve confidence and teamwork over time.

Our Responsibilities

Learn together. Improve together.

Within the BAF community, ETS helps make learning accessible and supports a positive training environment.

A

Training Sessions

Support planned lessons and activities that help members practise skills relevant to their in-game roles.

B

Mentoring & Feedback

Encourage constructive feedback, patience, and clear communication between instructors and trainees.

C

Community Standards

Promote respectful behaviour, reliability, and fair treatment throughout training and everyday activities.

Our Motto

“With courage and faith.” ETS values commitment, patience, integrity, and the willingness to keep learning. Strong teams are built when every member has the opportunity to improve.
